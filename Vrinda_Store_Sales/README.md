# Vrinda Store Annual Report 2022 – Excel Dashboard

## Project Overview
An interactive Excel dashboard that analyzes the 2022 sales performance of Vrinda Store, an online fashion retailer. It shows sales and order trends, customer demographics, regional performance, and sales channels in one view.

## Business Questions Answered
- How do sales and order volume change month by month?
- Who buys more, men or women, and which age groups?
- Which states generate the most revenue?
- Which sales channels bring the most orders?
- What share of orders are delivered, returned, cancelled, or refunded?

## Key Insights
- **Gender:** Women account for 64% of sales and men for 36%.
- **Age groups:** Adult women are the biggest segment (34.59%), well above adult men (15.47%).
- **Top states:** Maharashtra leads with 2.99M, followed by Karnataka (2.65M), Uttar Pradesh (2.10M), Telangana (1.71M), and Tamil Nadu (1.68M).
- **Channels:** Amazon brings 35% of orders, then Myntra (23%) and Flipkart (22%).
- **Order status:** 92% of orders were delivered, and only 8% were returned, cancelled, or refunded.
- **Trend:** Sales and orders peaked around March and declined gradually through the rest of the year.

## Tools & Techniques Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Data cleaning and formatting
- Pivot tables and pivot charts
- Slicers (Category, Channel, Month) for interactive filtering
- Combo chart, pie charts, and bar charts
